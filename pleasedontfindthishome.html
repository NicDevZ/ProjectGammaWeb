About us

Project Gamma is a community-driven roleplay server focused on long-form characters, consequential choices, and a city that keeps moving when you log off. We build custom systems in-house so the world feels coherent — not a pile of bolted-on scripts.

Rules, applications, and support all live on Discord. That is the hub for everything outside the game.

  • Character-first roleplay with room for serious and everyday stories
  • Custom economy and gameplay systems tailored to the server
  • An active staff and community presence on Discord
  • A living city meant to be shaped by the people who play in it

Contact

We do not use email or other contact channels. Join the Discord server for rules, support, applications, and everything else.
